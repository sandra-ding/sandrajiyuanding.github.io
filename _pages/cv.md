Research Experience
======
* Language & Cognitive Development Lab, UC Berkeley (PI: Mahesh Srinivasan)
  * Project 1: Discrepancies in Inferred Generic Relative Competence on Semantic and Linguistic Variation (2025)
    * _This study explores how children’s perceived relative competence of others is influenced by the recipients’ responses to novel versus commonsense knowledge._
    * Completed full data collection over two semesters (N = 90), administering behavioral experimental sessions with 4–8-year-old children across local museums and community field sites over one academic year.
    * Completed full R analysis on the current dataset and used multivariate model to explore age effect across conditions.
    * Facilitated onboarding process \& training spring 2025 RAs and presented findings at 2025 RA Presentations.

  * Project 2: Cross-linguistic Informativeness Biases in the Kinship Domain on Modifiers Use (Honors)
    * _This Honors project looks into how Hindi and Mandarin L1 speakers use modifiers in L2 kinship term.
    * Investigating whether native speakers of Hindi and Mandarin exhibit  informativeness biases when producing English kinship terms, reflecting L1-specific semantic accenting effect moderated by proficiency in L2 (English).
    * Recruited the full English native-speaking control adult and teen sample over Summer 2026; extending the original study's scope to Mandarin native speakers using a between-subjects design.
Designed the Mandarin speaker component of the study, operationalizing seniority and lineage as independent test dimensions to isolate the semantic features of Mandarin kinship terms that may complicate modifier use.
 
  * Project 3: Measuring and Intervening on the Effectiveness of Learning Novel Objects from Overhearing
    * _This study looks into how children learn novel object labels and object facts (respectively) through naturalistic environment._
    * Recruited and administered sessions with 3–5-year-old children both in-lab and at UC Berkeley Early Childhood Education Program (ECEP) school sites across a 7-week recruitment window, contributing to a cumulative dataset of N = 37.
    * Conducted accuracy and behavioral response coding, streamlined coding process through co-designing a data manual, helped revising scripts based on counterbalance analyses, and trained new RAs to conduct independent sessions.
    * Completed full R analysis on the current dataset and presented findings at 2026 Summer Internship Presentations.

* Family and Culture Lab, UC Berkeley (PI: Qing Zhou)
  * Project 1: Developing and Evaluating a English/Chinese Bilingual Dyslexia Psychoeducation Model
  *   _Independent Project, funded by UC Berkeley L&S Summer Undergraduate Research Fellowship_
  *   Designed and executed a two-phase mixed-methods study examining how Chinese-American caregivers and early childhood educators navigate the diagnostic overlap between second language acquisition and specific learning disabilities.
  *   Recruiting community stakeholders (N = 30) through partnerships with Yu Ming Charter School (Oakland) and Kai Ming Head Start (San Francisco); conducted semi-structured focus groups analyzed via inductive thematic analysis.
  *   Developing two culturally adapted psychoeducation modules for parents and educators grounded in Phase 1 qualitative findings and validated using pre-/post- measures of diagnostic self-efficacy and misconception endorsement.

  * Project 2: Early Childhood Education Workforce Analysis with ECE STEP Program, Kai Ming Head Start
    * Facilitated structured focus group interviews with early childhood educators in San Francisco's Chinese-American bilingual preschool community, documenting workforce barriers across Head Start-serving agencies.
    * Co-designed the IRB protocol for data collection, conducted onsite workforce observations, and wrapped up qualitative coding with inductive thematic analysis for focus group interviews and onsite observation field notes.

  * Project 3: Creating Culturally Adapted ADHD Psychoeducation Materials for Immigrant Families
    * Contributed to the cultural and linguistic adaptation of ADHD psychoeducation materials for Vietnamese- and Mandarin Chinese-speaking immigrant families, supporting translation workflows and coordinating community distribution.
